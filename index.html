I'm a developer who thrives at the crossroads of hardware and software. From soldering sensors to deploying full-stack dashboards, I build end-to-end systems that capture the world and make it legible.

My focus is biomedical IoT — real-time patient monitoring with microcontrollers, cloud pipelines, and clean interfaces that deliver critical data exactly when it's needed.
